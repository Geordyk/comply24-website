  • Export at any time

What the chain gives you

  • The scoring basis of every priority written out: legal weight, supervisory attention, risk.
  • Every judgement (covered, gap, out of scope) with a reasoning, a citation, a name and a time.
  • A change in the law reaches the objects it touches, and leaves the rest of the framework alone.
  • One export answers the supervisor's question per article.

The full profile

Institution Bank under DNB and ECB supervision

Scope Wft, Wwft, Sanctiewet, CRR and CRD, DORA, AMLR, EBA guidelines, DNB Good Practices, MiCAR where applicable

Existing tooling Risk platform for the register and incidents; alerting service; test workpapers in spreadsheets

  • News service
  • Risk and control register
  • Test evidence in files
  1. Three tools and no lineage: alerts in one, risks and controls in another, test evidence in files.
  2. SREP and DORA questions asked per article which control and which evidence covered it; the answer was a project each time.
  3. Key controls were tested, but residual risk was an opinion, not a result.
How it runs

From obligation to recorded decision.

1Scope, obligations and key controls

Platform

Scope per licence and activities. Obligations at provision level, merged and prioritised along the SREP structure. The Compliance Risk Assessor and the risk register score the controls; Finalize approves the key controls with reasons.

Professional

The second line approves; internal audit reads the scoring basis.

2Frameworks, policy house and risks linked

Platform

Control framework and policy house linked to obligations; gap assessment per document. The risk register holds the bank’s own risks and those from its risk platform, linked to controls; residual risk follows approved controls and test designs.

Professional

Owners validate the findings. The risk function keeps its register where it is.

3Test designs per key control

Platform

The ToD/ToE Creator generates a test design per key control from the control statement and its obligations: test of design, test of operating effectiveness, sample, frequency, evidence required. Approved and immutable per version.

Professional

Control owners and the testing team approve the design; internal audit can rely on it.

4Test and collect evidence

Platform

The control test environment runs the tests (existence, design, operating effectiveness) with evidence uploaded or linked, a version chain and a hash. Reports are frozen snapshots. A failed test opens a change.

Professional

Testers work from the plan; owners deliver evidence; findings are tracked to closure.

5Decision, follow-up and accountability

Platform

Radar, news and impact alerts on exactly the affected obligations, controls, policy clauses, risks and test designs. A change re-tests what changed. Deviations are re-approved when the law behind them changes. Dashboard with coverage by controls, policies and test designs, the delta table, change management and what is waiting on whom. Audit backlog per obligation with evidence. Supervisor pack per article.

Professional

Owners decide; AI proposes. The SREP information request becomes an export.

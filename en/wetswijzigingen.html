The rules behind it

Three rules that make the difference.

They decide what does and does not end up in your figures. Your second line wants to know them before trusting a coverage percentage.

  1. Only approved and selected frameworks count.A framework still being built does not enter your coverage figures.
  2. An approved deviation stays out of scope, until the law behind it changes.Then it comes back to whoever approved it at the time.
  3. A future version counts from the day it applies.Not from publication, and not from the entry into force of the instrument as a whole.

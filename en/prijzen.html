Two modules, one chain, your choice

Module 1

Regulatory Intelligence

The law translated into your obligations; frameworks and policy house linked and kept current.

  • Legislation at provision level, scope, obligations
  • Framework and policy building, policy clause tracking
  • Gap assessment and resolvement
  • News, radar, impact alerts, change process
  • Dashboard and audit backlog

Works on its own. Control Testing can be added at any time; the test designs attach to the same obligations and controls.

+

Completely integrated when used together: one data model, one lineage from law to evidence, one price list. No connector, no project.

Module 2

Control Testing

Key controls tested with evidence, in your own environment.

  • ToD/ToE Creator: test designs per control
  • Control test environment: existence, design, operating effectiveness
  • Evidence with version chain and hash, frozen reports
  • Findings to closure
  • Residual risk from the tests

Works on its own with your existing control framework. Regulatory Intelligence can be added later; the chain then runs from the law to your tests.

Three sizes, for either module or both

The size describes your institution, not the modules. Every size includes the full module.

Compact

Smaller institutions with one licence, one entity and a compact second line.

  • Full module scope, one entity
  • Expert seats for the second line; readers free
  • Fixed-price implementation in one phase
Recommended
Institution

Banks, insurers and pension funds with a full second line.

  • Full module scope, one entity
  • More expert seats; policy, control and test owners included
  • Risk platform linked
Group

Several entities or licences under one roof.

  • Multiple entities with shared and entity-specific scope
  • Group-level dashboard and export
  • Implementation per entity

Implementation

Fixed price, fixed scope, acceptance criteria. Your frameworks, policies, risks and test plans are loaded, not retyped. Adding the second module later is a short implementation, not a new project.

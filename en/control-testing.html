C24 Prove · Control Testing

From control to tested evidence.

Key controls get a test design, an execution and evidence with a version chain. A failed test opens a change, a passed test is confirmed. All in the same chain as the obligation the control comes from.

Test designs are generated per control. Tests and evidence live in the integrated control test environment: existence, design, operating effectiveness. Reports are frozen snapshots you can hand to an auditor. Residual risk follows from what has been tested, not from what is written.

What C24 Prove does

Testing that is demonstrable.

The test design is derived from the control, approved by a second pair of eyes and then locked per version.

  • Policy house: Template Merger and Policy Creator, updated when a change lands
  • ToD/ToE Creator: test designs generated per control, approved per version
  • Control test environment: existence, design, operating effectiveness
  • Evidence with version chain and hash; frozen reports
  • Findings to closure through the change process
  • Residual risk follows the tests, not the paperwork
  • Audit backlog per obligation with evidence; supervisor pack per article

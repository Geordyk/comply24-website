About us

Built by people from supervision and from the second line.

Why Comply24 exists

Alerting tools cover the front of the chain, GRC suites the back. The translation in between, from article to obligation to control, policy, risk and test, lives in neither. That translation is where we started.

How we work

Fixed price with acceptance criteria, no quote per project. One point of contact, no sales sequence. The outcomes are yours, as an export, also if you stop.

What we are not

Not a risk management platform and not a document system. Comply24 links to them; your risk register and your document management stay where they are.

First step

Start with one instrument.

Choose one instrument from your scope and share up to three policy documents. Fixed price, deductible from the implementation. The outcomes are yours, as an export.

  • The obligation framework for that instrument, with full lineage
  • A gap register per document, with finding, reasoning and citation
  • The change process demonstrated on one real change
  1. Scope and intakeInstrument, licence and documents; access to your environment set up.
  2. AssessmentObligations at paragraph level, gap findings per document with reasoning and citation, lineage.
  3. Findings session and handoverWalk-through with your second line, one real change demonstrated, export delivered.

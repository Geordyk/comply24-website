Signalling

The law moves. You see it first.

  • Sources read continuouslySupervisors, European authorities and the legislator publish every day. C24 Signal reads those sources, groups what is about the same subject and keeps the original publication with every item.
  • Applicability per institutionYour instruments and frameworks, weighted by licence and activities. What does not apply to you stays out.
  • Regulatory radarEvery law in your scope with its phase and the dates that matter: proposal, adopted, in force, applicable. A future version counts from the day it applies.
  • Weekly briefing, urgent within the hourOne readable message per week within your scope; a change with a short deadline arrives at once. Every claim links to the source.
How a signal becomes a change
What C24 Signal does

From publication to your scope.

C24 Signal signals. What you do with it is your work, and your responsibility; Regulatory Intelligence is where that work gets an owner, a deadline and evidence.

  • Instruments and frameworks

    Dutch and European regulation, guidelines and standards, per instrument with its version.

  • Applicability and weighting

    Determined per institution by licence and activities; the basis for the scope in C24 Trace.

  • Supervisor and legislator sources

    DNB, AFM, EBA, EIOPA, ESMA, the EU and the Dutch legislator, read continuously.

  • One item per subject

    Publications on the same subject are merged; the original source stays attached.

  • Radar with phases and dates

    Proposal, adopted, in force, applicable. An approaching date triggers a notice.

  • News settings and profiles

    Who receives what and how often; a profile per role or department.

  • Traceable to the primary source

    Every claim links to the original publication. Nothing is made up.

  • Handed over to C24 Trace

    An applicable change becomes an impact alert on the provisions it affects.

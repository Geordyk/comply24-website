Privacy

Privacy statement.

What we do with the data you leave through this website, and what you can expect from us.

Version of 13 October 2026

Who we are

Comply24 B.V., Gansstraat 170, Utrecht, Chamber of Commerce no. 42175762, is the controller for personal data processed through this website. Privacy questions: info@comply24.nl.

What data we process

Forms. When you request a demo, the C24 Health Check, the price list or security documentation, we process what you enter: name, institution, business e-mail address, the subject and your message, and for the Health Check the licence type and the requirement of your choice.

Visiting the website. The website uses no analytics, no tracking and no cookies that follow you. Our hosting provider keeps technical logs (such as IP address, time and requested page) to keep the website secure and available.

Purpose and legal basis

We use your data only to respond to your request and, if you wish, to prepare an appointment or proposal. Legal basis: taking steps at your request prior to a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

We do not use your data for newsletters or other marketing unless you ask for that separately.

Who receives the data

Form submissions are forwarded through Web3Forms and arrive in our business mailbox. The website is hosted by Vercel. Processor arrangements apply to these parties. We do not sell data and do not share it with other parties unless required by law.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to handle them and for at most twelve months afterwards, unless an agreement follows. In that case the retention periods of that agreement and statutory (tax) periods apply.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict processing, and you can object. Send your request to info@comply24.nl; we respond within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Security

This website is served over HTTPS. How we handle data inside the platform (a dedicated environment per institution, EU hosting, audit trail) is described on the Security and trust page. For customers we conclude a data processing agreement.

Changes

We may update this statement. The date at the top shows the latest version.

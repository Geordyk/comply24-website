How it runs

From obligation to recorded decision.

1Know the framework

Platform

Licence, entity and activities set the scope. Every instrument in scope at provision level; every provision an obligation, merged and prioritised with the scoring basis written out.

Professional

The legal counsel confirms the scope and the out-of-scope decisions, each with a reason.

2Link the policy house

Platform

Policies, standards and work instructions are loaded from SharePoint and assessed per obligation: covered, gap or out of scope, with reasoning and citation. The clause that covers an obligation is recorded: policy clause tracking.

Professional

The compliance officer confirms, corrects or rejects each finding. The gaps become changes with an owner.

3Signal what changes

Platform

Weekly news within the scope. The radar shows every instrument’s stage and key dates. A new version of an instrument raises impact alerts on exactly the obligations and policy clauses it touches, with the AI’s two-sentence reason.

Professional

The owner validates the alert. Nothing moves without a person.

4Act on it

Platform

A validated alert group becomes one change: owner, optional approver, plan, actions, evidence, review. The clause is updated and re-assessed. An approved deviation is re-approved when the law behind it changes.

Professional

Policy owners work from their task list; the compliance officer monitors the SLA.

5Prove it

Platform

The dashboard shows coverage of obligations by policies, open and closed changes per obligation, and what is waiting on whom. One export answers the supervisor per article. The in-control statement is a report, not a survey.

Professional

The board reads one page per quarter.

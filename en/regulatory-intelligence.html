From signal to closed change

A change arrives. Your chain closes it.

  • Impact alerts on exactly the affected objectsA changed provision raises an alert on each obligation, control, policy clause, risk and test design it touches. AI proposes what is affected and why, in two sentences; your owner decides.
  • One change processOwner, optional approver, plan, actions, evidence, review. An approved deviation is re-approved when the law behind it changes. Everything in the log, exportable.
  • Gap assessment and gap decisionsEvery policy document assessed against the obligations: covered, partial or gap, with citation and rationale. A gap becomes a change with an owner; a deliberate deviation is recorded as a decision.
  • Signal from Horizon ScanningThe radar and the weekly news live in C24 Signal. C24 Trace picks up what applies to you and turns it into work with an owner.

What this makes possible

From obligation to closed change.

Everything C24 Trace records sits as data in your own environment, with source, citation and version. Every change that comes in runs along the same chain until it is closed.

  • Obligations with an explanation

    Rationale, context and the link back to the original source.

  • Linked to your policy documents

    A change lands on the policy clause it affects.

  • Controls linked to obligations

    Built from the SCF control library and sector frameworks.

  • Citation and context per obligation

    The exact citation and the scoring basis are written out.

  • Full history and versions

    Changes to regulation, policies and controls stay traceable.

  • Gap assessment per document

    Gaps are closed through the change process, with an owner and evidence.

  • Dashboard and reporting

    Coverage, deltas, change management and the audit backlog.

  • Impact alerts with an AI proposal

    A change hits exactly the obligations, controls and clauses involved. The owner decides.

  • Change process with owner and SLA

    Approver, deadline and evidence; a change only closes with evidence.

  • Risk register linked to controls

    Own risks, your risk platform or CSV.

Who it is for

For the people a supervisor asks “show me”.

Compliance officers, risk managers, internal auditors, CISOs and board members at institutions supervised by DNB, the AFM and the ECB. Below, per type of institution what is at play, and under that what it delivers per role.

For which institutions?

For financial institutions that need demonstrable control of regulation.

Supervised by DNB or the AFM and, for significant banks, by the ECB through the SSM.

Prudential, conduct, integrity and operational resilience, with SREP-oriented prioritisation.

Compliance officer

From legwork to direct insight.

You know which regulation applies to your institution, what it asks of you and which policy documents hang off it. Without working it out from scratch every time.

Your challenge

You track which regulation applies and whether policy still matches it, often in spreadsheets alongside the documents themselves.

What Comply24 does

Obligations are recorded as data, linked to policy provisions. A change shows which documents it affects and what is still open.

How Comply24 supports this
Risk manager

From separate estimate to traceable residual risk.

Every risk hangs on the obligation it comes from and on the control that covers it. Residual risk follows from the test outcome.

Your challenge

You need to trace risks back to the obligation they come from and to the control that covers them.

What Comply24 does

Risks hang on the same chain as the obligation and the control. The scoring basis is written out, so a judgement can be followed.

How Comply24 supports this
Internal auditor

From building a file to retrieving one.

What was established, by whom, when and what the judgement rests on: it is already there. You retrieve it instead of reconstructing it.

Your challenge

You want to see what was established, by whom, when and what the judgement rests on, without rebuilding the file.

What Comply24 does

Every judgement is recorded with rationale, citation, name and time in an audit trail that can only be appended to. The export per article is the file.

How Comply24 supports this
CISO and security

From an action point in a report to a change with an owner.

A failed test does not get stuck in a report. It opens a change, with an owner, a date and the evidence that belongs to it.

Your challenge

DORA touches your technical measures, your suppliers and your incident process at the same time.

What Comply24 does

Key controls get a test design and evidence with a version chain and a hash. A failed test opens a change instead of an action point in a report.

How Comply24 supports this
Board & management

From a summary to evidence you can click through.

You do not just see that something is green, but what it rests on: the obligation, the test and the evidence record underneath.

Your challenge

You are accountable for control, but mostly see summaries that cannot be traced back to the source.

What Comply24 does

A dashboard that drills down to the underlying obligation, test and evidence record. What is open is open, with a date and an owner.

How Comply24 supports this
